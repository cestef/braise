**Math Module**

Provides mathematical utilities.

**Functions:**

- `rand(min, max)` - Generate a random number between min and max
- `abs(x)` - Get absolute value of x
- `sqrt(x)` - Get square root of x
- `sin(x)` - Get sine of x (in radians)
- `cos(x)` - Get cosine of x (in radians)
- `tan(x)` - Get tangent of x (in radians)
- `floor(x)` - Get the largest integer less than or equal to x
- `ceil(x)` - Get the smallest integer greater than or equal to x
- `round(x)` - Round x to the nearest integer
- `ln(x)` - Get natural logarithm of x
- `log10(x)` - Get base-10 logarithm of x
- `log2(x)` - Get base-2 logarithm of x
- `log(x, base)` - Get logarithm of x with specified base
- `exp(x)` - Get e raised to the power of x
- `pow(base, exponent)` - Raise base to the power of exponent
- `min(a, b)` - Get the minimum of two numbers
- `max(a, b)` - Get the maximum of two numbers
- `clamp(value, min, max)` - Clamp value between min and max bounds
- `lerp(start, end, t)` - Linear interpolation between start and end by factor t
- `hypot(x, y)` - Get the hypotenuse of a right triangle with sides x and y

**Fields:**
- `PI` - The mathematical constant π (3.14159...)
